Title: SOC Lab - Network Scan and Traffic Analysis

1. Nmap Scan:

I performed a network scan on my local system using Nmap.

The scan identified an open port:
- Port 9050 (tor-socks service)

This indicates that a proxy service is running on the system.

---

2. Wireshark Analysis:

I used Wireshark to capture network traffic during the scan.

I observed TCP and TLS traffic between my system (192.168.0.113) and external servers.

The communication used port 443, which indicates secure HTTPS traffic.

This helped me understand how network communication happens in real-world environments.
